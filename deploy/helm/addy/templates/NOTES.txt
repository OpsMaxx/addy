  Domain    {{ .Values.domain }}
  Portal    {{ if .Values.portal.enabled }}on{{ else }}off (headless -- the default){{ end }}
  Relay     {{ if .Values.turn.enabled }}embedded, advertising {{ .Values.turn.publicIP }}; 61000-65535/udp must reach this pod there{{ else }}NONE. Devices on networks that need one will not connect{{ end }}

BEFORE IT CAN SERVE, two things have to be true and neither is Kubernetes'
to arrange:

  1. {{ .Values.domain }} resolves to this Service's external address.
  2. Port 80 is reachable from the internet. ACME HTTP-01 arrives there or not
     at all, and a certificate that cannot be issued is a relay that cannot be
     reached.

The pod is Not Ready until its certificate is issued -- that is expected, and
the Service still carries the challenge to it. While it waits, addy says why
in its log once a minute:

    kubectl logs {{ include "addy.fullname" . }}-0 | grep -A6 'TLS is not being served'

(Not `addy preflight`: it proves port 80 by binding it, and in the running pod
addy already holds it.)

Once it is Ready, prove it actually works, rather than that it started:

    kubectl exec -it {{ include "addy.fullname" . }}-0 -- /addy selftest -connect 127.0.0.1:443 -data /var/lib/addy

Then mint the invite your first device needs, and in OpsMaxx open Sync &
devices, choose "This is my first device", and give it https://{{ .Values.domain }}
and the invite:

    kubectl exec -it {{ include "addy.fullname" . }}-0 -- /addy invite
