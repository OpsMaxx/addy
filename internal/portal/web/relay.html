Everybody who signs in sets up their own devices and mints their own invite — that needs nothing from you. This decides only who is let through the door in the first place, and it takes effect at once without restarting the relay.

Sync groups

Everything this relay knows: an identifier, a device count, some bytes. It holds no key, so there is nothing else for it to know — not a device name, not a server, not a file. Devices are added and removed from OpsMaxx, where the keys are.

Roster entries are not devices. An entry is a change to a device list, and a removal adds one rather than taking one away — so the number goes up when somebody removes a machine. Counting live devices means verifying the chain, which needs a key this relay does not have.
