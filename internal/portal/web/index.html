This is an addy relay. It is working.

Never type your recovery phrase here

Your recovery phrase is entered in the OpsMaxx desktop app and nowhere else — not in this page, not in any browser, for any reason. This page has no field for it, and a page that asks you for it is an attack.

For the same reason, compare your device-list fingerprint between two OpsMaxx windows rather than reading it here. An integrity check reported by the thing being checked is not a check.

Connecting a device

Your first device needs an invite from whoever runs this relay. Paste it into OpsMaxx along with this address, and it creates your account.

Every device after that is added by pairing: open OpsMaxx on both machines, one shows a code, and you check the same seven emoji appear on each. No invite, and nothing to type here. The relay cannot add a device by itself and never sees your data.

If you have lost every device, your twelve-word recovery phrase gets you back in — in the OpsMaxx app, never on this page.

Running this relay

If this relay is yours, the operator console is where you create invites and see how many accounts and devices are on it. It shows counts and sizes and nothing else: this server holds no key, so there is nothing else for it to show.
