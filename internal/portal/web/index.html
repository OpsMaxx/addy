Relay up

A relay that holds no key.

This is somebody’s addy relay. It carries messages and files between OpsMaxx devices, sealed before they ever arrive — so this server cannot read anything it stores, and neither can whoever runs it.

Have an invite?

Open OpsMaxx and paste it there, along with this relay’s address. That sets up your first device and creates the sync group your other devices will join.

There is no field for it on this page, and there never will be. An invite is typed into the app, not into a browser.

Never type your recovery phrase here

Your recovery phrase is entered in the OpsMaxx desktop app and nowhere else — not in this page, not in any browser, for any reason. This page has no field for it, and a page that asks you for it is an attack.

For the same reason, compare your device-list fingerprint between two OpsMaxx windows rather than reading it here. An integrity check reported by the thing being checked is not a check.

Getting your devices on

  1. Invite

    Your first device needs an invite. Once whoever runs this relay has admitted you, your console makes one for you; or they can hand you one. It creates the sync group every later device joins.

  2. Pair

    Every device after that is added by pairing: both apps open, one shows a code, and you check the same seven emoji appear on each. No invite, and nothing to type here.

  3. Recover

    If you have lost every device, your twelve-word recovery phrase gets you back in — in the OpsMaxx app, never on this page.

What this relay can see

  • Nothing of what you send — it is sealed before it reaches here
  • No names your device names are sealed too
  • Counts and sizes, because that is all that is left

The relay cannot add a device by itself, and signing in to the console does not change any of this. There is no key here to sign in to.

Running this relay

If this relay is yours, sign in to your console: an operator gets a card there on to the operator view, which is where you create invites for people who will not sign in and see how many sync groups are on the relay and how much each one stores. It shows counts and sizes and nothing else: this server holds no key, so there is nothing else for it to show.
